Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled *"ColdChainRx: A Blockchain-IoT-ML Approach to Pharmaceutical Cold Chain Integrity"* for consideration for publication. 

The integrity of pharmaceutical cold chains is a critical global challenge, particularly highlighted by the recent distribution requirements for temperature-sensitive mRNA vaccines. While existing literature has proposed integrating blockchain and IoT for supply chain tracking, these systems predominantly focus on backend logistics. 

Our work introduces **ColdChainRx**, a novel framework that bridges this gap by fusing a Hyperledger Fabric permissioned ledger, active IoT telemetry, and an Isolation Forest anomaly detection model. Our defensible edge is the integration of a consumer-facing QR verification portal that democratizes access to cold-chain integrity status, allowing patients and healthcare providers to detect counterfeit or thermally compromised drugs instantly at the point of care.

Our performance evaluation demonstrates a robust throughput of ~650 TPS with 85ms latency, confirming the system's viability for regional distribution networks. We believe this work aligns perfectly with your focus on applied blockchain technologies and IoT systems. 

This manuscript has not been published previously, nor is it under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have approved the final manuscript and agree with its submission to this venue.
